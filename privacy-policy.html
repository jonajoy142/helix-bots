Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this WhatsApp Support Bot demonstration application handles information received through WhatsApp.

1. About This Application

This application is a demonstration customer-support chatbot that integrates with the WhatsApp Business Platform. It is intended for development, testing, and demonstration purposes.

2. Information We Receive

When you send a message to the WhatsApp number connected to this application, the application may receive information such as:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • The message you send
  • Message timestamps and identifiers
  • Information required to process and respond to your request

3. How Information Is Used

Information received through WhatsApp is used only to process incoming customer-support requests and generate responses from the chatbot.

The application may use an AI language model to understand messages and generate appropriate responses.

4. Data Storage

During development and testing, conversation messages and application data may be stored locally for debugging, demonstration, and application functionality.

This demonstration application does not intentionally sell personal information or use WhatsApp message data for advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

The application uses the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta to receive and send WhatsApp messages.

Depending on the configured AI provider, messages may also be processed by an AI model provider or a locally hosted language model.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used during development to protect application data. This application is currently a development and demonstration system and should not be treated as a production enterprise service.
